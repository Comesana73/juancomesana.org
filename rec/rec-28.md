## Registration

There is no registration fee for the conference, but please notify Adrian Liu, the conference manager, if you plan to attend by sending an email to [rutgersepistemologyconference@gmail.com](mailto:rutgersepistemologyconference@gmail.com). There will be a conference dinner on Friday and a lunch on Saturday and we hope that everyone participates in the meals. Participation in meals will cost $100 total if you are faculty and postdocs and $70 total if you are a graduate student or an undergraduate. You can pay for the meals via e-check (if you have a bank account in the US) or credit card otherwise. If you do have a US bank account, we ask that you use the e-check option to help us with the associated fees. In either case, you can use [this link](https://nam02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fquikpayasp.com%2Frutgers%2Fcommerce_manager%2Fpayer.do%3ForderType%3DPhilosophy_EpistemologyConf&data=05%7C01%7Clesperance%40philosophy.rutgers.edu%7Cd70fceecfd994bc0619508dbf114159b%7Cb92d2b234d35447093ff69aca6632ffe%7C1%7C0%7C638368841472639875%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C3000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=krCQV4DT5PuzYSppLVftIUk6GRCW8m7IqP9XsDKVSkM%3D&reserved=0) to pay. When you visit that link, you will be asked to enter your email address and then click on the verification link you will receive. ## Where to stay

All sessions will be held at [The Heldrich Hotel](https://www.theheldrich.com). A limited number of reduced-priced rooms are available to those attending the conference. The reduced rate and booking link will be posted here.

If you are a graduate student or a postdoc and would like to attend the conference and stay with a Rutgers graduate student, please contact the conference manager at [rutgersepistemologyconference@gmail.com](mailto:rutgersepistemologyconference@gmail.com). We will try to provide all graduate students and postdocs a place to stay, but we cannot make any promises.

## How to get there

**Plane & Train:** If you are flying, it is best to fly into Newark Airport. It is about 25 miles from The Heldrich. The best way to get from the airport to New Brunswick is via NJ Transit. The train stops at the airport and it is a 25 min train ride from the airport to New Brunswick. When you arrive at Newark Airport, follow the signs to the monorail “airtrain”. The airtrain will take you to the NJ transit train stop. Trains run from Newark Airport to New Brunswick about every half hour. A one-way ticket Newark Airport – New Brunswick is about $20. You can buy tickets at the vending machines at the Newark Airport train station or on the mobile app NJ Transit. The Heldrich is a 5 min walk from the New Brunswick train station.

**Train:** The best way to get to New Brunswick from New York or Philadelphia is via NJ Transit. The Heldrich is a 5 min walk from the New Brunswick train station.
