The labs

Interactive tools for the course. They run in any web browser, on a laptop or a phone, with nothing to install.

  • Logic Lab: truth tables, syntax trees, and translations into PL (Part I).
  • Probability Lab: stochastic truth tables, the probability axioms, Bayes’ theorem, and eikosograms (Part II).
  • Decisions & Games Lab: decision matrices under ignorance and under risk, and two-player games (Part III).
